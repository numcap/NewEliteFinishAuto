## book.html — booking submission now redirects to our own thank-you page
Iterated to a bulletproof solution because Formspree's free plan kept showing its
own `formspree.io/thanks` page (it ignores the `_next` redirect):
- Final approach: the **Submit button is `type="button"`** (so the form can never
  do a native POST to Formspree), and a **click handler** validates the fields,
  sends the booking to Formspree in the **background (fetch/AJAX)**, then
  **redirects to `thank-you.html`** on our own domain.
- Removed the old inline "Thank you for your submission" success block.
- Kept the `_next` hidden field as a harmless no-JS fallback.
- Field validation preserved (required fields via `reportValidity()`, plus the
  "choose a package" check).
