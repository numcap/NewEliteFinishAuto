HOW TO ADD YOUR BEFORE / AFTER PHOTOS
=====================================

Just drop your photos into THIS folder and rename them to the exact
names below. Then refresh the website — they appear automatically.

Slider 1:  before-1.jpg   and   after-1.jpg
Slider 2:  before-2.jpg   and   after-2.jpg
Slider 3:  before-3.jpg   and   after-3.jpg

TIPS
----
- Names must match exactly: lowercase, a hyphen, the number, ending ".jpg"
- For each slider, shoot the BEFORE and AFTER from the same angle so they
  line up when you drag the slider.
- A 4:3 shape (e.g. 1200 x 900 pixels) fits best. Bigger is fine — the
  photos are auto-cropped to fit the box.
- Keep each file under ~500 KB so the page loads fast.
  (You can shrink photos for free at https://tinypng.com)

Using PNG / HEIC / WEBP instead of JPG?
Either convert them to .jpg, or tell Claude and the website can be
updated to use your format.
