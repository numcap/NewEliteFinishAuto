How often should you detail your car?

Elite Finish Auto · Mobile detailing in London, Ontario

One of the most common questions we get is simple: how often should you actually detail your car? The honest answer is that it depends on how you use your vehicle. Here is a practical schedule for London, Ontario drivers.

The general rule

For most drivers, a full detail every 4 to 6 months, with lighter maintenance cleans in between, keeps a vehicle looking and smelling its best and protects its resale value.

Factors that change how often you should detail

  • Kids: spills, crumbs and sticky messes mean interiors need attention more often.
  • Pets: pet hair and odour build up fast and benefit from regular interior cleans.
  • Commuting: high-mileage cars collect more road grime, bugs and brake dust.
  • Winter: our salt-heavy season is hard on both paint and carpets.

A simple seasonal schedule

  • Spring: a full detail to remove winter salt and built-up grime and reset the car for the year.
  • Summer: an exterior detail with a fresh coat of protection against sun and bugs.
  • Fall: an interior deep clean and a protective sealant before winter arrives.
  • Winter: regular washes plus an interior salt extraction as needed.

Interior vs exterior

Exteriors benefit from more frequent washes and a sealant top-up to protect the paint. Interiors usually need a deep clean a few times a year, sooner if you have kids or pets. Our Cabin Care, Showroom Shine and Full Detail packages let you choose exactly what your car needs.

Signs you are overdue

  • The interior smells stale or musty
  • Stains or salt marks on carpets and seats
  • Water no longer beads on the paint
  • Built-up bugs, tar or brake dust you can feel
