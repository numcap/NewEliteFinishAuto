FAQ

Mobile detailing questions

Do you really come to my home?

Yes. Elite Finish Auto is a fully mobile service. We arrive at your driveway across London and the surrounding areas — including our core North London neighbourhoods of Masonville, Fox Hollow, Sunningdale and Hyde Park — and complete the entire detail on-site.

What do you need from me?

Just access to a standard power outlet and an outdoor water tap. We bring all equipment, products and supplies.

How long does a full detail take?

A Showroom Shine or Cabin Care takes around 60–90 minutes. A Full Detail typically takes 2.5–3.5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Add-ons such as iron decontamination add extra time.

Do you remove winter salt stains?

Absolutely. Salt removal is part of our interior deep clean and is especially important for North London winters. We extract salt from carpets and mats and treat door sills and floors.

Which areas do you serve?

We mainly serve North London, Ontario, but we also cover all of London and the surrounding areas. Our core neighbourhoods are Masonville, Fox Hollow, Sunningdale and Hyde Park. Call us to confirm coverage.
